⚄ DICEBOUND THE EMBER ROAD
THE EMBER ROAD · v1.0Original pixel sprites. A little luck. Your next move.

Small dice. Big trouble.

Lead three heroes through three bosses. An event always comes before the next fight.

  1. Move for free. Pick a hero, then a glowing tile. Each hero has 2 steps per round. You can split those steps.
  2. Spend your rolled dice. Each hero rolls twice from their six-face pool. Each card is one action. Click a ready card to use it. Attacks hit the boss automatically when in range. No mana, no extra payment.
  3. Dodge the red tiles. They show exactly where the boss will hit when you end the round. Read the plan above the cards for its exact damage.
  4. Bad roll? Reforge all unused Blanks once per round. A Blank can also become a 1-damage Pebble, or an extra step if you enable Blank Step.
  5. End round. The boss acts, then everyone rolls again. Guard lasts through that boss attack. Heal a fallen hero to revive them.

Keyboard: 1 2 3 select hero · Q / W dice · R reforge · E end round · Esc cancel.

The game saves events, battles and rewards automatically on this browser. Continue shows your event receipt before resuming a fight. New Adventure always starts with a fresh event.
